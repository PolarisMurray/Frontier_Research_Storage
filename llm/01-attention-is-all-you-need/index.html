LLM / 01 · NeurIPS 2017

Attention Is All You Need

Translation models used to pass information step by step through RNNs or CNNs, with attention as a helper. This paper removes recurrence and convolution, leaving only attention.

之前的翻译模型靠 RNN 或 CNN 逐步传递信息，注意力只是辅助。这篇论文去掉循环和卷积，只留下注意力。

Vaswani, Shazeer, Parmar, Uszkoreit, Jones, Gomez, Kaiser, Polosukhin arXiv:1706.03762

Abstract

摘要（译文）

The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks that include an encoder and a decoder. The best performing models also connect the encoder and decoder through an attention mechanism. We propose a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely. Experiments on two machine translation tasks show these models to be superior in quality while being more parallelizable and requiring significantly less time to train. Our model achieves 28.4 BLEU on the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task, improving over the existing best results, including ensembles, by over 2 BLEU. On the WMT 2014 English-to-French translation task, our model establishes a new single-model state-of-the-art BLEU score of 41.8 after training for 3.5 days on eight GPUs, a small fraction of the training costs of the best models from the literature. We show that the Transformer generalizes well to other tasks by applying it successfully to English constituency parsing both with large and limited training data.

目前主流的序列转换模型都基于复杂的循环或卷积神经网络，包含一个编码器和一个解码器。表现最好的模型还通过注意力机制把编码器和解码器连接起来。我们提出一种新的简单网络架构 Transformer，它完全基于注意力机制，彻底摒弃了循环和卷积。在两个机器翻译任务上的实验表明，这类模型质量更高，同时更易并行，训练时间也大幅减少。我们的模型在 WMT 2014 英译德任务上达到 28.4 BLEU，比现有最好结果（包括 ensemble）高出 2 BLEU 以上。在 WMT 2014 英译法任务上，模型在 8 块 GPU 上训练 3.5 天后，创下 41.8 BLEU 的单模型新纪录，训练成本只是文献中最好模型的一小部分。我们还把 Transformer 成功用于英语成分句法分析，无论训练数据多还是少都表现良好，说明它能很好地推广到其他任务。

01 How information flows01 信息怎么流动
Recurrent layers take O(n) sequential steps, and distant words are O(n) hops apart
Self-attention connects every position in O(1) sequential steps, with a maximum path of O(1)
循环层：n 个词要走 O(n) 个顺序步，相距很远的两个词之间隔着 O(n) 跳
自注意力：一层之内所有位置直接相连，O(1) 个顺序步，最长路径 O(1)
02 The role of attention02 注意力的角色
Attention works alongside a recurrent network and produces one distribution at a time
Attention is the whole model, with h = 8 heads running in parallel on different representation subspaces
注意力配合循环网络使用，每次算出一种关注分布
注意力就是主体：h = 8 个头在不同的表示子空间里并行计算
03 The scoring formula03 打分公式
Dot-product attention: q·k grows with d_k, pushing softmax into regions with tiny gradients
Scaled dot-product: q·k / √d_k
点积注意力：d_k 变大后 q·k 也变大，softmax 进入梯度极小的区域
缩放点积：q·k / √d_k
04 Where word order comes from04 顺序信息从哪来
Order is implicit in the structure: recurrence steps through time, convolution slides a window
The structure carries no order, so sin/cos positional encodings are added to the embeddings
顺序隐含在结构里：循环按时间步推进，卷积按窗口滑动
结构不再带顺序，用 sin/cos 位置编码加到词向量上
